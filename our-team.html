Our Team

Meet the Radiologists

Behind every report is a team committed to accuracy, compassion, and patient-centered care. Meet the radiologists helping guide Clearview Radiology Associates’ diagnostic imaging services.

Dr. Edgecombe

Radiologist

Grounded in advanced academic training and evidence-based medicine, Dr. Edgecombe brings a focused interest in oncologic and breast imaging to her radiology practice.

Read Full Bio Show Less

Grounded in more than 15 years of diagnostic imaging experience, Dr. Edgecombe brings a wealth of clinical expertise, academic training, and a commitment to excellence in patient care.

She completed her residency training in Radiology at The University of the West Indies (UWI) and subsequently earned a Master’s degree in Oncoimaging from the University of Pisa, Italy. Her advanced training and extensive clinical experience have helped shape a practice grounded in accuracy, compassion, and evidence-based medicine.

Her areas of clinical and academic interest include oncologic imaging and breast imaging, with a particular focus on breast cancer screening and the evolving role of medical imaging in the early detection and management of cancer. She is passionate about the important role radiology plays in providing timely and accurate diagnoses and supporting patients throughout their healthcare journey.

She is dedicated to delivering high-quality, patient-centered diagnostic imaging, combining clinical expertise with the latest evidence and advances in radiology. Her ongoing involvement in professional development reflects her commitment to advancing the standard of radiological care in The Bahamas.

Dr. Johnson-Minnis

Radiologist

A Consultant Radiologist with broad experience across public and private healthcare, Dr. Johnson-Minnis combines clinical leadership, mentorship, and compassionate service.

Read Full Bio Show Less

A Consultant Radiologist with over 15 years of experience across both the public and private sectors, Dr. Talietha Minnis brings broad expertise in ultrasound, X-ray, CT, MRI, breast and MSK imaging, as well as image-guided procedures, particularly breast biopsies.

She currently serves as Director of Radiology at Princess Margaret Hospital and is actively involved in the training and mentorship of medical students, residents, and imaging professionals.

In addition to her public-sector leadership, Dr. Minnis provides radiology services in private practice, with a strong focus on delivering timely, accurate, and patient-centered diagnostic care. She is passionate about making medical imaging more accessible, improving radiology services throughout The Bahamas, and ensuring that every patient is treated with compassion, professionalism, and respect.

For Dr. Minnis, radiology is more than a profession; it is part of her ministry. She believes that caring for patients is an opportunity to serve, provide reassurance, offer answers, and make a meaningful difference in each person’s healthcare journey.

Dr. Brooks

Radiologist

With more than 15 years of experience in radiology, Dr. Brooks brings strong clinical knowledge, professional dedication, and a patient-centered approach to diagnostic imaging.

Read Full Bio Show Less

With more than 15 years of experience in radiology, Dr. Brooks brings a strong foundation of clinical knowledge, professional dedication, and patient-centered care to Clearview Radiology Associates.

His academic background includes The University of the West Indies, St. Augustine, where he developed the foundation for his medical and radiological training. Throughout his career, he has continued to build extensive experience in diagnostic imaging and clinical practice.

Dr. Brooks has also served at Princess Margaret Hospital for 16 years, gaining valuable experience within one of the country’s major healthcare institutions. His years of service have contributed to a deep understanding of patient care, diagnostic accuracy, and the important role imaging plays in supporting physicians and guiding treatment decisions.

At Clearview Radiology Associates, Dr. Brooks remains committed to providing high-quality imaging services with professionalism, care, and attention to detail. His experience and dedication support the facility’s mission of delivering accurate results in a comfortable and patient-focused environment.
